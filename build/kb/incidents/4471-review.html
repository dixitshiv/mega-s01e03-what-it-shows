Separate engineering work

Engineering tickets: shared parser and booking-v3 hint.

Shared parser — day-first input read month-first. For a tenant whose locale writes day first, the shared parser reads dates month first. For example, Harbour Dental’s app report R-41200 describes 7 April appearing as 4 July. Use the tenant locale, preserve the intended calendar date, and verify generated invites and reminders. Check app, email and booking-v2 paths; a booking-v3 tenant’s app or email report still belongs here under the supplied classifier.

Booking-v3 hint — displayed format disagrees with the form reader. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY but reads input in the tenant’s day-first order. Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour run this form. Copper Studio’s form report R-41230 describes 6 January appearing as 1 June even though intake called it parser. Make the hint, input validation and interpretation agree with the tenant locale. Fixing the shared parser alone leaves this path misleading.

For regression checks, use an ambiguous date such as 6 January and an unambiguous day such as 23 January. Check both stored values and the invite recipients actually see; do not silently swap dates or infer intent from the browser locale.
