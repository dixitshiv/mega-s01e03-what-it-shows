0002 · Merge only with evidence of the same cause

owner dixitshiv proposed reviewed 2026-11-01 · 1 days ago

Context

The review of #4471 shows that a matching symptom does not imply one failing path. The rendered examples already compare cause while the old prose tells staff to compare subjects. Accepted decision 0001 stays unchanged as history.

Proposed replacement

Read the report and look up its tenant locale and form. Merge only when there is evidence of the same cause and affected path. A subject match is a search aid. Neither a subject match nor an intake tag establishes a duplicate. When tenant data is missing or the symptoms conflict with the classifier, keep the report for investigation rather than guessing.

Consequences

Triage requires reading and tenant lookup. It keeps separate fixes visible and gives engineering a count with evidence. Counts deduplicate by report ID. Record anomalies alongside their current assignment, and revise the assignment when investigation establishes a different cause.

This record proposes replacing the subject-only rule. Desk approval is still required; its status does not claim that approval has happened.
