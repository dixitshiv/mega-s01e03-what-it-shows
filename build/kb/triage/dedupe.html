The rule needs replacement

Decision 0001 accepted matching subjects in 2024. It is preserved as history. The rendered examples below contradict that shortcut: matching subjects can have different causes and different subjects can share one cause.

Proposed decision 0002 documents the replacement; desk approval remains pending.

  1. Read the body and look up the tenant’s locale and form.
  2. Identify the failing path and compare it with an existing issue’s evidence.
  3. Merge only with evidence of the same cause; deduplicate report IDs when counting.
  4. Keep uncertain cases for investigation. A missing tenant is no basis for merging, even with identical subjects.
An intake tag is a guess from browser locale. Use tenant data, and investigate descriptions that the cause classifier cannot explain.
